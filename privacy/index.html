Privacy

Last updated October 2026

Signal is a product of Actuality Live Inc. This page explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you fill out our form, we collect the information you enter: your name, work email, company website, and your answers about your firm and how you find work. We also record how you found us, such as the link or campaign that brought you to the site.

How we use it

We use your answers to get back to you, to understand whether Signal fits your firm, and, if you ask for it, to send you a weekly email of matched projects. We don't sell your information.

Emails and unsubscribing

We only send the weekly digest if you choose to receive it. Every digest includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can also reply to any email from us to stop receiving them.

Your information

To see, correct or delete what you've shared with us, reply to any email from us and we'll take care of it.
