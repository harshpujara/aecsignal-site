The problem

Most firms hear about a project the day its RFP is posted. By then the client usually has a favourite, and it’s the firm they’ve been talking to for months.

How it works

Every public project leaves a paper trail. Signal starts reading at page one.

A school, fire station or office building shows up in public documents long before it reaches a bid portal. Signal follows it through each step, so your team has time to build the relationship while everyone else is still waiting for the RFP.

01Budget line A capital plan sets money aside for a new building or upgrade, with a rough cost and a target year. Signal flags it here
02Council approval A staff report lays out the scope, budget and timeline, and elected officials vote on it.
03Procurement planning The owner decides how to buy, whether that's an RFQ, a shortlist or design-build.
04RFP posted The project finally appears on a bid portal, with a deadline a few weeks out. Most firms find out here

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

What is Signal?

Signal finds upcoming AEC projects early. It reads public records like capital budgets, council agendas, rezonings and permit filings, and sends your team the projects that fit your firm, so you can start building the relationship before the RFP is out.

Who is Signal for?

Business development and proposal teams at architecture, engineering and construction firms, and anyone else whose work depends on hearing about projects early.

Where does the information come from?

Public sources: municipal and school board capital budgets, council and board agendas, staff reports, funding announcements, rezoning and development applications, building permits and procurement portals. Every project links back to the document it came from.

How is this different from a lead service?

Most lead services show every firm in a region the same list. Signal filters projects to the work your firm actually does and tells you why each one fits. And because it comes from Actuality, the projects you find can turn into proposals you write in Actuality.

Which regions do you cover?

We’re starting with a focused set of markets in Canada and the US. Tell us where you work when you sign up and we’ll let you know if we cover it.

What does it cost?

The weekly digest is free for your first four weeks. If it’s useful, we’ll talk about pricing after that.
